Why an assessment?

Make the next data investment a better decision.

Disconnected systems, Excel reporting, manual reconciliation and conflicting KPIs make it difficult to know what to fix first. The assessment connects the business problem to its economic impact, then establishes a practical sequence of action.

Seven dimensions

What SDM investigates

Business & Data Strategy

Alignment between data capability and business objectives.

Data & Information

Critical data, ownership, accessibility and structure.

Architecture & Technology

Systems, integrations, platforms and constraints.

Data Quality

Completeness, accuracy, consistency, timeliness and validity.

Governance

Ownership, policies, security, lineage and accountability.

Analytics & BI

Reporting, semantic models, KPIs and decision support.

People & Operating Model

Skills, responsibilities, processes and operating capability.
