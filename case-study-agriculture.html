Cloud Data Infrastructure

Agricultural Data Ingestion & Tracking Portal.

The Operational Paradigm

The client, a regional agricultural operation, relied entirely on vulnerable, manual paper records to manage complex farm logistics and livestock inventory. This analog approach introduced severe data latency, high risks of data loss, and fundamentally prevented leadership from making agile, data-driven decisions regarding operational health and resource allocation.

Cloud Architecture & Deployment

To modernize their operations without the overhead of heavy enterprise software, I engineered a custom, lightweight cloud database application that fully digitized data collection from the field directly to the executive dashboard.

  • Front-End Data Ingestion: Developed custom, mobile-responsive data entry interfaces utilizing Google Apps Script, providing field operators with direct, secure API endpoints for immediate data capture.
  • Backend Cloud Storage: Engineered automated pipelines to push sanitized, validated form data directly into structured, relational backend tables hosted in Google BigQuery, ensuring high availability and secure scalability.
  • Real-Time Visualization: Connected the BigQuery data warehouse directly to Looker Studio. Built an automated dashboard to deliver real-time inventory tracking and operational metrics to stakeholders.
  • Operational Impact: Achieved a 100% elimination of paper-based tracking and manual entry lag, transitioning the client to a fully automated, cloud-native operational portal.
