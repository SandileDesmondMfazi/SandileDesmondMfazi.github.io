Cloud Data Infrastructure

Eliminated 100% of manual paper tracking by engineering a real-time, cloud-native agricultural data portal.

Client Snapshot

IndustryAgriculture & Livestock Management
RegionSouthern Africa
EngagementCloud Data Architecture
TimelineStrict deadline delivery finalized in June 2026

Business Challenge

  • Field operations relied entirely on highly vulnerable, manual paper records to manage complex farm logistics and livestock inventory.
  • Analog data collection caused severe reporting latency between the field and the executive office.
  • Manual aggregation introduced high risks of data loss, corruption, and tracking inaccuracies.
  • Leadership fundamentally lacked the immediate operational visibility required to make agile resource allocation decisions.

Objectives

  • Completely digitize field data collection without introducing heavy enterprise software overhead.
  • Establish a secure, scalable cloud storage backend.
  • Deliver real-time inventory tracking and operational metric dashboards to stakeholders.
  • Eliminate the manual data entry bottleneck entirely.

Approach

  • Discovery: Mapped field operator workflows to identify critical data collection touchpoints and constraints.
  • Architecture: Designed a lightweight, serverless cloud architecture to ensure high availability while minimizing infrastructure maintenance for the client.
  • Implementation: Engineered custom API endpoints bridging mobile-responsive input forms directly with structured cloud tables.
  • Adoption: Configured automated, self-refreshing dashboards for executive stakeholders to drive immediate operational pivots.

Solution Delivered

  • Custom Mobile-Responsive Data Ingestion Forms (Google Apps Script).
  • Relational Cloud Data Warehouse (Google BigQuery).
  • Direct Data API Endpoints.
  • Real-Time Executive Operational Dashboard (Looker Studio).
