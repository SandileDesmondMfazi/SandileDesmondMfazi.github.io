Cloud Data Infrastructure

Eliminated 100% of manual field tracking by engineering a real-time, zero-maintenance cloud data portal for livestock logistics.

Client Snapshot

IndustryAgriculture & Livestock Management
RegionSouthern Africa
EngagementCloud Data Architecture
TimelineStrict deadline delivery finalized in June 2026

Business Challenge

  • Field operations relied entirely on highly vulnerable analog paper records to manage complex farm logistics, feed consumption, and livestock inventory.
  • Physical data collection caused severe multi-day reporting latency between remote fields and the central executive office.
  • Manual aggregation and environmental damage to paper records introduced high risks of data loss, corruption, and chronic inventory discrepancies.
  • Leadership fundamentally lacked the immediate operational visibility required to make agile resource allocation and supply chain decisions.

Objectives

  • Completely digitize field data collection utilizing offline/online capable mobile interfaces.
  • Bypass the immense overhead and licensing costs associated with traditional enterprise ERP systems.
  • Establish a secure, serverless cloud storage backend requiring zero hardware maintenance.
  • Deliver real-time inventory tracking and operational metric dashboards to stakeholders.

Approach

  • Discovery: Mapped field operator workflows to identify critical data collection constraints, ensuring the UI required minimal technical literacy to operate efficiently outdoors.
  • Architecture: Designed a lightweight, serverless cloud architecture. This circumvented the need for the client to provision or maintain physical IT infrastructure.
  • Implementation: Configured custom Google Apps Script logic as a secure middleware layer, validating data inputs before pushing them via API directly into structured, relational BigQuery tables.
  • Visualization: Connected the data warehouse to Looker Studio, configuring automated, self-refreshing dashboards for executive stakeholders to drive immediate operational pivots.

Solution Delivered

  • Data Ingestion: Custom Mobile-Responsive Forms built via Google Apps Script.
  • Data Warehouse: Enterprise relational cloud storage hosted in Google BigQuery.
  • Integration: Direct, secure Cloud API Endpoints.
  • Analytics: Real-Time Executive Operational Dashboards built in Looker Studio.

Key Outcomes

Manual WorkAchieved a 100% elimination of paper-based tracking and manual data entry errors.
Data LatencyReduced reporting time from 72+ hours to sub-60-second cloud synchronization.
VisibilityProvided leadership with an immediate, automated view of complex livestock logistics and feed metrics.
InfrastructureDeployed a fully serverless portal, bringing hardware maintenance costs to zero.
