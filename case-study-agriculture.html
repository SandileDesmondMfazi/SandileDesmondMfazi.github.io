Cloud Data Infrastructure

Eliminated 100% of manual field tracking by engineering a real-time, zero-maintenance cloud data portal for livestock logistics.[cite: 5]

IndustryAgriculture & Livestock Management[cite: 5]
RegionSouthern Africa[cite: 5]
EngagementCloud Data Architecture[cite: 5]

The Situation

Field operations relied entirely on highly vulnerable analog paper records to manage complex farm logistics, feed consumption, and livestock inventory.[cite: 5] Physical data collection caused severe multi-day reporting latency between remote fields and the central executive office.[cite: 5]

Why this engagement mattered

Manual aggregation and environmental damage to paper records introduced high risks of data loss, corruption, and chronic inventory discrepancies.[cite: 5] Ultimately, leadership fundamentally lacked the immediate operational visibility required to make agile resource allocation and supply chain decisions.[cite: 5] They needed to digitize collection while bypassing the immense overhead and licensing costs associated with traditional enterprise ERP systems.[cite: 5]

My Approach

  • Discovery & Workflow Mapping: Mapped field operator workflows to identify critical data collection constraints, ensuring the UI required minimal technical literacy to operate efficiently outdoors.[cite: 5]
  • Cloud Architecture: Designed a lightweight, serverless cloud architecture to circumvent the need for the client to provision or maintain physical IT infrastructure.[cite: 5]
  • Integration Middleware: Configured custom Google Apps Script logic as a secure middleware layer, validating data inputs before pushing them via API directly into structured, relational BigQuery tables.[cite: 5]
  • Visualization: Connected the data warehouse to Looker Studio, configuring automated, self-refreshing dashboards for executive stakeholders to drive immediate operational pivots.[cite: 5]

The Outcome

Three Values in Action

Curiosity

I audited the exact physical environments where data was collected to ensure the UI design accounted for outdoor glare and minimal technical literacy.

Integrity

Rather than upselling a costly, bloated ERP system, I engineered a highly targeted cloud architecture that strictly answered the client's operational needs.

Sustainability

The entire pipeline is serverless. The client requires zero IT staff to maintain servers, patch hardware, or manage database uptime.

Architectural Decision Record (ADR)

Strategic Rationale: Serverless BigQuery vs. Traditional ERP

When designing this portal, I actively chose a Serverless Google Cloud architecture (Apps Script + BigQuery) over deploying a traditional on-premise farm management ERP.

This decision bypassed prohibitive enterprise licensing fees and eliminated the need for the client to provision local servers. By routing validated inputs through secure Cloud API Endpoints into BigQuery[cite: 5], the data scales infinitely while remaining highly relational and structured for Looker Studio visualization.[cite: 5]
