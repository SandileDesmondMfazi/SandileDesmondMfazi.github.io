Enterprise Data Architecture

Recovered 115 million records of lost operational history and automated enterprise reporting across three countries to establish a singular source of truth.

Client Snapshot

IndustryMultinational Assurance & Financial Services Provider
RegionSouthern Africa (Botswana, South Africa, Zambia)
EngagementEnd-to-End Business Intelligence Consulting
Data Volume200+ Million Rows

Business Challenge

  • The organization's legacy transactional databases overwrote historical contract states rather than versioning them, actively destroying the lifecycle history of policies.
  • The destruction of historical data prevented actuaries and executives from conducting accurate churn, vintage, and retention analyses.
  • Regional operations across Botswana, South Africa, and Zambia were constrained by disparate, siloed data sources.
  • Reporting required heavy, manual Excel compilation, causing severe bottlenecks and conflicting KPI values across national branches.

Objectives

  • Mathematically reconstruct the lost historical data to recover the full lifecycle of every policy.
  • Centralize data from three countries into a single, verifiable Windows Server environment.
  • Automate regional reporting to eliminate manual Excel processes entirely.
  • Deploy specialized, role-based analytical cubes to enable near real-time KPI monitoring for claims, sales, and collections.

Approach

  • Discovery & Logic Mapping: Embedded directly with business representatives to map operational processes and define exact metric logic prior to development, ensuring high user adoption.
  • ETL & Storage Architecture: Engineered an orchestration layer of over 30 SSIS packages to extract data via ODBC. Designed a specialized relational snapshot database specifically to rebuild historical contract states by parsing flattened audit trails.
  • Semantic Modeling: Constructed highly optimized SSAS tabular models compartmentalized by business function. Implemented complex DAX measures to track dynamic currency conversions (Pula FX adjustments), time-intelligence (YTD, SPLY), and multi-tier arrears bucket migrations.
  • Governance & Presentation: Deployed a centralized SSRS Native-Mode web portal. Enforced data sovereignty by implementing rigid Row-Level Security (RLS) across the semantic models, ensuring regional stakeholders only access data pertinent to their specific country.

Solution Delivered

  • ETL Pipeline: Automated SSIS packages managed via SQL Server Agent.
  • Data Warehouse: Centralized SQL Server 2019 Enterprise instance.
  • Analytical Cubes: SSAS Tabular Models deployed for Claims & Premiums, Collections, Sales, and Policy Acquisition.
  • Delivery Portal: SSRS automated subscriptions delivering paginated and mobile-optimized executive reports.
