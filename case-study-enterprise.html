Technical Breakdown

Enterprise Data Warehouse & Analytics Rollout.

The Organizational Paradigm

The client, a multinational assurance and financial services organization operating across three countries, was severely constrained by disparate, siloed data sources[cite: 13]. This fragmented architecture caused severe reporting bottlenecks and highly vulnerable, manual Excel entry processes.

A critical operational flaw compounded these issues: the legacy transactional database overwrote historical contract states rather than versioning them, actively destroying the lifecycle history of policies and preventing accurate churn or vintage analyses[cite: 13].

Structural Data Architecture

To eliminate these bottlenecks and recover lost operational history, I architected and deployed a fully automated, end-to-end Microsoft BI pipeline establishing a singular, verifiable source of truth managing over 200 million rows of data[cite: 13].

  • Extraction & Transformation (ETL): Engineered an orchestration layer of over 30 SSIS packages to securely extract data from the operational source databases via ODBC[cite: 13]. Applied in-stream data-quality rules and standardized the data into conformed staging schemas[cite: 13].
  • History Reconstruction & Warehousing: Designed a specialized relational snapshot database specifically to reconstruct lost policy lifecycles[cite: 13]. This required parsing over 115 million raw, flattened audit log records to mathematically rebuild the historical state and transition type of every contract over time[cite: 13].
  • Semantic Layer (SSAS Tabular Cubes): Constructed highly optimized SSAS tabular models compartmentalized by business function, including Claims & Premiums, Policy Acquisition, Sales, and Collections[cite: 13]. Implemented complex DAX measures to track dynamic currency conversions, time-intelligence (YTD, SPLY), loss ratios, and multi-tier arrears bucket migrations[cite: 13].
  • Visualization & Output (SSRS): Deployed a centralized, native-mode reporting architecture[cite: 13]. This delivered a mix of high-concurrency paginated reports, mobile-optimized KPI dashboards, and live cube-connected Excel workbooks for financial analysts[cite: 13].
  • Governance & Security: Enforced data sovereignty across the multi-national operation by implementing rigid row-level security (RLS), ensuring regional stakeholders could only access data pertinent to their specific country or branch[cite: 13]. Delivery was fully automated via role-based email subscription schedules aligned to finance timelines[cite: 13].
