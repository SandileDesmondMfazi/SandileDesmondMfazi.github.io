Enterprise Data Architecture

Recovered over 200 million records of lost operational history and automated enterprise reporting across three countries to establish a singular source of truth.

Client Snapshot

IndustryMultinational Assurance & Financial Services Provider
RegionSouthern Africa (Botswana, South Africa, Zambia)
EngagementEnd-to-End Business Intelligence Consulting
Data Volume200+ Million Rows

Business Challenge

  • The organization's legacy transactional database overwrote historical contract states rather than versioning them, actively destroying the lifecycle history of policies.
  • The destruction of historical data prevented accurate churn, vintage, and retention analyses.
  • Regional operations were constrained by disparate, siloed data sources.
  • Reporting required heavy manual Excel compilation, causing severe bottlenecks and conflicting KPI values across national branches.

Objectives

  • Reconstruct the lost historical data to recover the full life of every policy.
  • Create a single, verifiable source of truth across all three national territories.
  • Automate regional reporting to eliminate manual Excel processes.
  • Enable near real-time KPI monitoring for executives regarding claims, sales, and collections.

Approach

  • Discovery: Embedded directly with business representatives to map operational processes and define exact metric logic prior to development.
  • Architecture: Designed a specialized relational snapshot database specifically to mathematically rebuild historical contract states from raw, flattened audit trails.
  • Implementation: Built highly automated ETL pipelines via SSIS, deployed compartmentalized SSAS tabular models for specific business units, and established a centralized SSRS reporting portal.
  • Adoption: Delivered side-by-side user training to designated internal product owners and established strict, role-based security governance.

Solution Delivered

  • Automated SSIS ETL Pipelines (30+ Packages).
  • Reconstructed Historical Data Warehouse.
  • SSAS Tabular Cubes (Claims, Collections, Sales, Policy Acquisition).
  • SSRS Native-Mode Web Portal (Mobile & Paginated Executive Reports).
