Deployment Spotlight

Southern African Livestock Logistics Rollout

The Bottleneck

Field operations relied entirely on vulnerable analog paper records. Physical data collection caused severe multi-day reporting latency (72+ hours) between remote fields and the central executive office.

The Architecture

I mapped field operator workflows and designed a lightweight, serverless cloud architecture. Using Google Apps Script as middleware, field inputs were validated and pushed via API directly into structured BigQuery tables.

The Business Impact

Achieved a 100% elimination of paper-based tracking. Reporting latency was reduced from multi-day delays to sub-60-second cloud synchronization via Looker Studio dashboards.
