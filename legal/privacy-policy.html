Data Privacy

Transparent handling of your corporate data.

This policy defines exactly what information is collected when you utilize this website, how it is secured, and how it is utilized for B2B communication.

01. B2B Communication

Data collected via inquiry forms is strictly used to evaluate your business intelligence needs, prepare architectural blueprints, and facilitate direct professional dialogue.

02. Zero Third-Party Selling

I do not sell, rent, or lease your corporate email, name, or project constraints to any third-party marketing agencies, brokers, or external software vendors.

1. Information Collection

When you interact with the "Get Your Blueprint" portal or book a consultation, I collect standard B2B contact data. This includes your Full Name, Corporate Email Address, Company Name, and the operational details you provide regarding your current infrastructure constraints. This data is securely processed using standard web transmission protocols.

2. Third-Party Infrastructure

To facilitate secure communication and scheduling, this site leverages trusted third-party infrastructure. Inquiry forms are routed securely via Formspree, calendar bookings are managed via Calendly, and the site is hosted on secure servers. These entities act as data sub-processors and maintain their own enterprise-grade privacy and security standards.

3. Data Retention

Inquiry data and communication logs are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes of project scoping and potential contract execution. If an inquiry does not result in a formal engagement, contact details and project descriptions are periodically purged from my active communication pipelines.

4. Your Rights & Access

You have the absolute right to know what personal data I hold about you, the right to request corrections to inaccuracies, and the right to request the complete deletion of your data from my communication systems ("Right to be Forgotten"). To exercise any of these rights, please contact me directly at sandilemfazi.12@gmail.com.
