The SDM Value Framework™

How SDM Quantifies Value

We start by understanding the economic opportunity across time saved, revenue opportunity, cost reduction, risk reduction and decision velocity. Transformation pricing is then scoped according to complexity, delivery requirements and the capability being created—not an unsupported promise of ROI.

01. Time & Labour Savings

Quantifying and eliminating the hundreds of hours your highly-paid analysts spend manually exporting, cleaning, and consolidating Excel files every month.

02. Revenue Recovery

Identifying pipeline leakage, missed cross-sells, and churn risks that are currently invisible due to fragmented systems and reporting latency.

03. Risk Avoidance

Calculating the financial exposure of bad data governance, broken spreadsheets, and lack of Row-Level Security, then architecting systems to neutralize it.
