B2B Contracting Framework

A frictionless, globally compliant engagement model.

Engaging offshore data architecture expertise should not introduce legal or administrative overhead. My consultancy operates on a strict Business-to-Business (B2B) framework engineered for rapid onboarding with UK, European, and global enterprise clients.

1. Legal Framework & Data Sovereignty

Before any internal infrastructure is assessed, a mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) is executed to ensure absolute operational confidentiality. Project scopes, deliverables, and timelines are then legally codified through a standard Master Services Agreement (MSA) and a project-specific Statement of Work (SOW). Data sovereignty is strictly maintained; client data is never hosted on unauthorized external servers.

2. UK IR35 Compliance (Outside IR35)

For UK-based organizations, my services strictly fall Outside IR35. I operate as an independent, offshore commercial entity providing specialized, project-based architectural deliverables—not as a disguised employee. This mitigates all localized payroll tax liabilities and compliance risks for your organization.

3. Cross-Border Billing & Invoicing

Financial logistics are highly streamlined. Invoicing is conducted on a monthly or milestone basis, rendered natively in GBP (£), EUR (€), or USD ($). To eliminate international wire friction and compliance hurdles, payments can be seamlessly routed through global Contractor Management platforms such as Deel, Oyster HR, or via direct B2B transfer utilizing Wise for Business.

4. Asynchronous Global Delivery

Operating from Gaborone, Botswana (GMT+2), my timezone aligns perfectly with the UK and Europe for real-time daily stand-ups and collaborative architecture reviews. Furthermore, this alignment allows for highly efficient asynchronous delivery: heavy data pipeline processing, ETL orchestration, and semantic model deployments are executed during off-peak hours, ensuring dashboards are fully updated and stabilized prior to your executive team's morning review.
