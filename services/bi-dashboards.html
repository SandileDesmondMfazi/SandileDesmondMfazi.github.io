The Symptoms of Fragmented Reporting

If you lack a canonical semantic layer, it usually looks like this.

01. Metric Chaos

Different departments, different results.

Because calculations are isolated in individual analysts' spreadsheets, "Gross Margin" means something completely different to Sales than it does to Finance.

02. Decision Latency

Executives waiting on manual reports.

By the time operations managers compile, validate, and email the weekly performance Excel sheets, the data is already 72 hours out of date.

The Implementation

How I Build Your Analytics Layer

PHASE 1

Metric Standardization

I audit your current reports to locate where logic diverges, stripping out fragmented calculations and aligning stakeholders on concrete business definitions.

PHASE 2

Governed Semantic Models

PHASE 3

Executive Visuals

I deploy interactive Power BI and Looker Studio dashboards secured with strict Row-Level Security, ensuring automated, daily visibility across your organization.
