Frequently Asked Questions

Do we have to pay per-user licensing fees for these portals? +
That depends strictly on the tech stack we deploy. If we build using Google Apps Script, it leverages your existing Google Workspace with zero extra per-user cost. If we use Power Apps, it utilizes your existing Microsoft 365 licenses. This strategy bypasses the massive seat-licensing fees typical of traditional enterprise ERPs.
Will these portals work on mobile devices in the field? +
Absolutely. Every portal interface I engineer is designed "mobile-first" specifically for field operators. I account for outdoor screen glare, large touch targets, and low technical literacy to ensure 100% adoption by your front-line teams.
Where does the data actually go when a user hits submit? +
The portal itself does not permanently store data. It acts as a secure, validation gateway. Once the data passes logical checks, it is pushed directly via API into your centralized cloud warehouse (like BigQuery or SQL Server), where it is instantly available for reporting.

What Happens Next?

Your field data is automated and your operational tools are connected. The next move is feeding all that new data directly into executive dashboards so leadership has real-time KPI visibility.
