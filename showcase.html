Executive Summary

Client Challenge

The finance team lacked visibility into regional profitability because sales, marketing, and inventory data were siloed in entirely separate legacy systems.

Business Objective

Create a single source of truth allowing executives to monitor profitability, revenue growth, and regional performance automatically without manual Excel intervention.

Solution Delivered

  • Integrated PostgreSQL, MariaDB, and CSV marketing data into a central hub.
  • Designed a dimensional data warehouse.
  • Built a reusable Power BI semantic model.
  • Deployed automated executive KPI dashboards.

Key Insights

  • Overall revenue increased 14% Year-over-Year.
  • Northern region net margins declined 3.5%.
  • Aggressive, untracked promotional discounting reduced overall profitability.

Business Recommendation

Introduce strict regional pricing controls and automate daily margin monitoring to protect profitability while maintaining the current trajectory of revenue growth.
