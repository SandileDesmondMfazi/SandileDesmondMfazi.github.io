UK Retail & E-Commerce Financials

Executive Analytical Report

1. The Business Problem

The executive board lacked visibility into regional margin degradation and could not accurately track Year-over-Year (YoY) product performance due to fragmented, legacy systems.

2. Available Data

Three years of raw transactional history comprising 1.5 million rows spanning PostgreSQL (e-commerce backend), legacy MariaDB (in-store POS), and flat CSV files (marketing spend drops).

3. Analytical Approach

Engineered an SSIS pipeline to centralize the disparate systems into a Kimball star-schema data warehouse. Developed a robust DAX semantic layer to mathematically normalize currency formatting and pre-calculate complex time-intelligence metrics prior to dashboard visualization.

4. The Most Important Finding

While overall gross revenue grew by 14% YoY, actual net margins in the Northern UK territories contracted by 3.5% due to aggressive, untracked promotional discounting on flagship product lines.

5. Recommendations & Next Steps

Recommendation: Standardize regional discounting guardrails immediately to protect margins.
Next Steps: Implement automated, daily Power BI margin alert triggers for regional directors and integrate live warehouse inventory data to optimize stock replenishment for high-margin items.

Under The Hood: End-to-End Pipeline

ETL Architecture Diagram

1. Data Engineering (SSIS)

Transactional data is extracted from heterogeneous databases via ODBC. SSIS packages perform in-stream data quality checks, handle missing values, and execute UPSERT commands into the staging warehouse.

Star Schema ERD

2. Dimensional Modeling

Designed following Kimball methodology. The central `Fact_Sales` table is surrounded by heavily conformed dimensions (`Dim_Date`, `Dim_Product`, `Dim_Store`), ensuring instantaneous slice-and-dice query performance.

Semantic Layer DAX

3. Semantic Layer (DAX)

The SSAS Tabular model mathematically pre-calculates time-intelligence metrics and enforces Row-Level Security (RLS) so regional managers only view data strictly tied to their operational territory.
